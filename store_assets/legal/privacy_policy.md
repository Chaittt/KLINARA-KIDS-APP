> Version 1.0 · Effective 16 October 2026

This policy explains how **Klinara Kids Pre-School, Hyderabad** ("Klinara Kids", "we", "the school") collects, uses and protects personal data in the **Klinara Kids** mobile app. The app is used by parents of enrolled children and by school staff (teachers, administrators and the Managing Director). Children do not use the app themselves.

## 1. Data we collect
| About | Data |
|---|---|
| Parent / guardian | Name, mobile number, app username, an encrypted (hashed) password, consent choices, complaints and messages you submit, fee payment references (UPI transaction ID) you enter. |
| Child | Name, age, class, photo (optional), attendance, daily activity notes and photos posted by teachers, homework, fee plan and payment status, and Elthea GROW assessment results (only if you opt in). |
| Staff | Name, work email, role, class, attendance and salary records. |
| Device | A push-notification token so we can send school alerts. We do not collect location, contacts, advertising IDs or browsing history. |

## 2. Why we use it
Only to run the pre-school:
- sharing your child's attendance, activities, homework, schedule and announcements with you
- managing admissions, fees and receipts
- staff administration
- answering complaints
- keeping the app secure

We do **not** show ads, sell data, or use data for profiling or marketing. We do not track or monitor children's behaviour beyond normal classroom records.

## 3. Who can see it
- **You** see only your own child(ren)'s information.
- **Teachers** see the children in their class.
- **Administrators and the Managing Director** see school-wide records needed to run the school.
- **Service providers** handle the app on our instructions and may not use the data for their own purposes. They host the app (cloud servers and database), deliver push notifications, and, only if you opt in, run **Elthea** (section 4).

We share data with authorities only when Indian law requires it.

## 4. Elthea AI assessment (optional)
Elthea is a third-party developmental assessment ("GROW": Grow, Relate, Observe, Wonder).
- **It's off by default** and runs only if a parent turns it on in the app.
- **What Elthea processes:** your child's first name, age and class, plus the assessment answers. Elthea's servers may be located outside India.
- **Results:** stored in Klinara Kids and visible to you and to school staff.
- **Turning it off:** go to *Profile → Privacy* at any time. No new assessments will run, and you can ask us to delete earlier results.

## 5. How long we keep data
- **Account, child profile, attendance, activities and Elthea results:** kept while your child is enrolled. They are deleted within **30 days** after you request deletion or after your child leaves, unless you ask us to keep them.
- **Fee invoices and payment records:** kept for up to **8 years**, because Indian tax and accounting laws require it. They are kept only for that purpose.
- **Security logs** (such as failed-login counters): kept for a short time only.

## 6. Security
- Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
- Access is limited by role, and accounts are locked after repeated wrong passwords.
- If a personal-data breach happens, we will inform affected parents and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

## 7. Your rights
Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:
- access a summary of your and your child's data
- correct or update it
- withdraw consent (for example, by turning Elthea off)
- ask for erasure
- nominate another person to act for you
- raise a grievance

Requests are answered within 30 days.

## 8. Deleting your account and data
You can ask for deletion in any of three ways:
- In the app: *Profile → Delete my account*
- The public form: `https://<your-server>/api/delete-account`
- Email: admissions@klinarakids.com

The Managing Director verifies the request. Within 30 days, they erase the parent account and the child's app data: profile, photos, attendance, activities, complaints and Elthea results. Fee records are kept only as described in section 5.

## 9. Children
Children's data is collected only from their parent or guardian, at admission, for the child's education and safety. We ask for a parent's consent in the app before use, and separately for Elthea.

## 10. Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the version and date above and show a notice in the app.
